# Homework Assignments

The homework assignments in your student workbook are a supplement to your in-class lessons. Completing these homework assignments is an expectation as part of the self-study portion of this course.

This workbook include some homework that is tied directly to **assignments for marks**. See the [LICENSE](../LICENSE.md) for further information.

> ⚠️ **WARNING:** While you are encouraged to use AI to help you learn, you are *not* permitted to use AI on your for-marks assignments in this course. Using AI on for-marks assignments is considered **academic misconduct**.

The remaining self-study homework assignments may or may not contain starter kit files in your initial copy of this workbook; these may be provided by your instructor through targeted clones (e.g.: *tiged*) or other means (zip files, Brightspace downloads, etc.).
