# SDEV1150 Student Workbook

> *Edit the front-matter section at the top of this file with your personal information.*

This **Student Workbook** is your place to practice the concepts and material for this course. Please familiarize yourself with the [**license**](./LICENSE.md).

The material in this repository is divided into [In-Class Lesson Starter Kits](#in-class-lessons) and corresponding [Homework Assignment Starter Kits](#homework-assignments). It is your reponsibility to keep the material in your student workbook up-to-date over the course of this semester.

At the end of the term, the online copy of this workbook will be deleted and you will not be able to push/synchronize with the online repository. Since subsequent courses may build on the material in this course, you are encouraged to retain this workbook on your computer as your personal reference material after the course is completed.

> ⚠️ **WARNING:** While you are encouraged to use AI to help you learn, you are *not* permitted to use AI on your for-marks assignments in this course. Using AI on for-marks assignments is considered **academic misconduct**.

### Reading Links

Readings do not have to be completed prior to your next class. However, you are responsible to keep up with these assigned readings in this course. **Quizzes and exams** (as the theory component of the course) will be based on these assigned readings as well as in-class lessons/lectures.

The reading links are intended to support what you are learning from your in-class lessons and to prepare you for quizzes. They explain the meaning and workings of JavaScript in contexts related to the in-class lessons. They also offer technical details and concrete examples.

1. Official MDN Documentation
