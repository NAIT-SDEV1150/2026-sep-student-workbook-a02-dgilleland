# Assignment 1

> - Weight: **20 %** of the final course mark
> - Points: **26** points (max)

This is a **formative evaluation**. In it, you will demonstrate your grasp of the JavaScript fundamentals. The assessment consists of several small in-class and homework (self-study) assignments (bucket assignments). All of these are to be performed in and submitted through your student workbook for the course. Awards for the completion of the bucket assignments will be based on timeliness (completing them in/by the required periods) and automated tests.

There are 10 in-class<sup>†</sup> assignments and 5 homework<sup>‡</sup> assignments. Each are worth up to two points. The maximum earned points are 26. In the event that an in-class assignment cannot be delivered or that an approved extension is granted, that in-class assignment may be completed outside of scheduled class time.

## Rubric

Each assignment is worth two points, based on the following criteria:

- **Excellent (2 points)** - Assignment completed on time; automated tests all pass.
- **Acceptable (1 point)** - Assignment completed on time; some or all tests fail.
- **Incomplete (0 points)** - Assignment notably incomplete or submitted after its deadline.

Assignment types:
- <sup>**†**</sup> - In-Class assignment (directed-study)
- <sup>**‡**</sup> - Homework assignment (self-study)
