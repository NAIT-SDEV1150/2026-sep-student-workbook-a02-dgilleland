# In-Class Starter Kits

💫 The starter kits in this folder are coordinated with in-person class periods. The materials provided here are **NOT** a substitute for participating in scheduled classes. Part of the expectation for your enrolement in this course is class attendance and participation. 👀

This workbook include some in-class starter materials that are tied directly to **assignments for marks**. See the [LICENSE](../LICENSE.md) for further information.

> ⚠️ **WARNING:** While you are encouraged to use AI to help you learn, you are *not* permitted to use AI on your for-marks assignments in this course. Using AI on for-marks assignments is considered **academic misconduct**.

Additional materials may be provided by your instructor through targeted clones (e.g.: *tiged*) or other means (zip files, Brightspace downloads, etc.).

## Using This Workbook

As a **3 Credit** course, it should take approximately **135 hours** to complete. The scheduled in-class time is 4 hrs/week, which means only 60 hours are in-class and the remaining 75 hours are self-directed. Expect to do about 5 hrs/week of work *outside* of class!

More information about self-study homework assignments can be found in the [**homework** `ReadMe.md`](../homework/ReadMe.md).
