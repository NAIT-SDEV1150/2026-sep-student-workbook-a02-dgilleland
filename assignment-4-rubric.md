# Assignment 4

> - Weight: **20 %** of the final course mark
> - Points: **20** points (max)

This assignment is mostly a **formative evaluation** with a smaller portion being **summative** in nature. In it, you will demonstrate your grasp of developing custom elements in JavaScript with automated tests. The assessment consists of several small in-class and homework (self-study) assignments (bucket assignments). All of these are to be performed in and submitted through your student workbook for the course. Awards for the completion of the bucket assignments will be based on timeliness (completing them in/by the required periods) and automated tests.

There are 5 in-class<sup>†</sup> assignments and 5 homework<sup>‡</sup> assignments. Each are worth up to two points. The maximum earned points are 18. In the event that an in-class assignment cannot be delivered or that an approved extension is granted, that in-class assignment may be completed outside of scheduled class time.

## Rubric

Each assignment is worth two points, based on the following criteria:

- **Excellent (2 points)** - Assignment completed on time; automated tests all pass.
- **Acceptable (1 point)** - Assignment completed on time; some or all tests fail.
- **Incomplete (0 points)** - Assignment notably incomplete or submitted after its deadline.

<sup>**§**</sup> One assignment stands out from the others. It is worth **4 points**, as it is a *summative* assessment (all the others are formative assessments).

Assignment types:
- <sup>**†**</sup> - In-Class assignment (directed-study)
- <sup>**‡**</sup> - Homework assignment (self-study)
